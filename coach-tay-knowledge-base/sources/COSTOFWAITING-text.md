[¶1] One of the hardest things to see as a coach is a talented player who has BIG dreams but wasn't given the preparation she needed to reach them. Because the cost of waiting isn't always something you can put a dollar amount on. Sometimes, it's an opportunity your daughter never gets back. It's realizing too late that she spent years developing the wrong skills, attending events that weren't putting her in front of the right coaches, or never building the physical strength and mental toughness needed to compete at the next level. While other players were preparing, developing, and positioning themselves for opportunities, she was simply playing basketball and hoping someone would notice. And the heartbreaking part? She might have had every bit of the potential to earn those opportunities, but potential alone isn't enough. Waiting can mean missing out on scholarship offers, losing opportunities at schools she dreamed of attending, or having fewer options when it's time to make a decision. As a parent, you can't guarantee your daughter a scholarship, but you CAN help make sure she has the preparation, knowledge, and support to give herself the best possible chance. Starting early isn't about pressuring her to succeed. It's about making sure that when opportunity comes knocking, she's ready to answer.
