[¶1] DON'T WAIT UNTIL COACHES ARE WATCHING. We just talked about how hustle can be developed as a skill. But there's another piece of this that I think is even bigger.
[¶2] Don't wait until a college coach is sitting in the gym to become the player you think they want to see. Don't suddenly start diving on the floor because you know a coach is watching. Don't suddenly become the loudest teammate on the bench. Don't suddenly sprint everywhere. Don't suddenly have a great attitude. Don't suddenly start encouraging your teammates.
[¶3] Because here's the problem: These things are not a light switch. You can't turn them ON when a coach walks into the gym and turn them OFF when they leave. Eventually, who you really are is going to show.
[¶4] The goal isn't to look like a hard-working, positive, disciplined athlete. The goal is to become one.
[¶5] IT HAS TO BECOME A WAY OF LIFE. Hustle is a habit. Effort is a habit. Positivity is a habit. Discipline is a habit. Being a good teammate is a habit. Doing things the right way is a habit. And habits are built through repetition. That's why I want athletes to understand that developing these qualities doesn't only happen on a basketball court.
