[¶1] Once you start reaching out to college coaches, it can get confusing quickly. You might contact 20–30 schools, your high school coach may reach out to some, your AAU coach may contact others, and eventually you're asking: "Wait... did we already email this coach?" Don't try to keep all of this in your head.
[¶2] CREATE A SIMPLE GOOGLE SHEET. Name it: FIRST NAME LAST NAME — COACH CONTACT TRACKER. Keep track of: School; Coach first and last name; Coach title; Email; Phone number, if available; Date athlete first contacted; How you contacted them; HS coach contacted? YES / NO; AAU coach contacted? YES / NO; Response? YES / NO; Notes.
[¶3] Your notes can be extremely simple: Asked for film. Sent transcript 6/12. Coming to watch July 10. Needs a PG in 2028. No response yet. Follow up after next tournament.
[¶4] UPDATE IT AS YOU GO. Every time you contact a coach, record it. If they respond, update it. If your high school coach reaches out, update it. If your AAU coach calls someone, update it. This should take seconds.
[¶5] And remember: YOU CONTACTING A SCHOOL ≠ THAT SCHOOL RECRUITING YOU. Over time, this sheet will help you see which programs are actually responding, asking for information, watching you, and continuing the conversation. It also makes follow-up much easier because you know exactly what has already happened.
[¶6] Recruiting has too many moving pieces to rely on memory. DON'T JUST DO THE RECRUITING WORK. KEEP TRACK OF THE RECRUITING WORK.
