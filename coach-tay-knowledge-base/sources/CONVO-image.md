# CONVO-F · "Conversation Starters for Parents and Athletes" (image, transcribed)

- **Source:** Image pasted by the user, 2026-11-11 (Batch 4). Header: "Real questions. Meaningful conversations. A stronger connection." Side: "Ask · Listen · Learn · Discern." "Be intentional. Be curious. Be engaged." Footer: "Good questions lead to great opportunities."
- Questions for families to ask college coaches/programs.

**Program & Style of Play (understand the vision):** Can you tell us about your style of play? · How do you see my daughter fitting into your system? · What do you value most in your players? · How do you evaluate and develop players? · What are your expectations on and off the court? · What makes your program different from others? · How would you describe your team's culture?

**Coaching & Communication (build the relationship):** How do you typically communicate with players and families? · How do you give feedback to your players? · How do you handle challenges or conflict within the team? · What is your coaching philosophy? · Who will be involved in my daughter's development? · How often do players meet with you individually? · What can we do to be a great fit for your program?

**Academics & Support (plan for the future):** How does your program support players academically? · What majors are popular among your athletes? · Are there academic advisors specifically for athletes? · How do you help players balance basketball and school? · What resources are available if a player is struggling academically? · What is the graduation rate for your players? · Can you share some examples of recent graduates and what they're doing now?

**Opportunity & Development (invest in growth):** How do you develop players over time? · Can you give examples of players who have improved under your coaching? · How do you determine playing time? · What does the typical career path look like for a player in your program? · Do you have players who have gone on to play professionally? If so, can you share their journey? · How do you help players prepare for life after basketball?

**Campus & Community (picture the bigger life):** What do you love most about your school? · How would you describe the campus and surrounding community? · What do players typically do in their free time? · How is the support for women's basketball on campus? · Can you share a little about the team's involvement in the community? · What is it like to live in the dorms? · Will freshmen be required to have a roommate? Do players typically room together?

**Player Well-Being (support the whole person):** How do you support the mental and emotional well-being of your players? · What does a typical weekly schedule look like? · How do you handle injuries and rehab? · What does the summer look like for your players? · Do players get a break for Christmas? If so, how long? · Are there opportunities for players to be involved in other activities or organizations on campus? · How do you help players manage stress and pressure?

**Financial & NIL (get the facts):** Can you explain your scholarship opportunities? · What other forms of financial aid are available (including academic aid)? · How does NIL work at your school? · Do you support your players in NIL opportunities? · What should we know about the cost of attendance? · Are there resources to help players learn about financial literacy?

**Next Steps (know what to expect):** What does the recruiting timeline look like for you? · What are the next steps in the process? · Are there additional visits or opportunities to spend time with the team? · What information do you need from us? · Is there anything you'd like to see my daughter focus on right now? · When can we expect to hear from you? · What would make someone a great fit for your program?

**Open-Ended Questions (keep the conversation going):** What do you love most about coaching? · What is the most rewarding part of what you do? · What is one thing you wish more recruits asked you? · Can you share a story about a current or former player who represents what your program is about? · What are you most excited about for the future of your program? · Is there anything else you think we should know?
