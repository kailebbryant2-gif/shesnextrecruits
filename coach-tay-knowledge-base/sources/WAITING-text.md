[¶1] WHY WAITING TO COMMIT CAN BACKFIRE. There is a phrase I hear all the time in recruiting: "WE'RE WAITING TO SEE IF SOMETHING BETTER COMES." Be careful. Because while you're waiting... THE SCHOOL THAT WANTS YOU IS STILL RECRUITING. College coaches only have so many roster spots to fill. Maybe they need: One point guard. One post. Two wings. They cannot necessarily wait forever for one player to decide. So coaches may have multiple offers or active options out for the same roster need, depending on the program and circumstances. And sometimes: THE FIRST PLAYER TO SAY YES FILLS THE NEED. The other opportunities can change quickly.
[¶2] YOUR OFFER DOESN'T FREEZE THE RECRUITING BOARD. An offer doesn't necessarily mean: "This spot will be waiting for you whenever you're ready." Recruiting keeps moving. Players commit. Transfers happen. Roster needs change. Other recruits visit. Coaches adjust their boards. That's why one of the questions you should ALWAYS ask is: "COACH, WHAT IS THE TIMELINE ON THIS OFFER?" Know whether there is a deadline. Know whether they're recruiting other players at your position. Know whether your opportunity changes if another player commits.
[¶3] BE CAREFUL WAITING FOR "BETTER". Especially as recruiting gets later, ask yourself: What exactly am I waiting for? If School A has: Called consistently. Built a relationship. Watched you repeatedly. Brought you to campus. Answered your questions. Made you a clear offer. Told you exactly how they see you fitting... And you're holding them off because you're hoping School B eventually becomes interested... PAY ATTENTION TO THAT DIFFERENCE. School A is showing you that you're a priority. School B may still be deciding.
[¶4] AND SOMETIMES YOU MOVE UP SOMEONE ELSE'S BOARD. This happens in recruiting. A program may have another player higher on its board. They pursue her. She commits somewhere else. Now the staff moves to the next player on its board. That doesn't automatically mean the program wouldn't value you or that you'd have a bad experience there. Recruiting boards move constantly. But it IS information you should understand. Ask yourself: "HOW LONG HAS THIS PROGRAM BEEN SHOWING ME THAT THEY REALLY WANT ME?" Because there's a difference between a coach who has consistently recruited you... and one who suddenly became extremely interested after other options disappeared.
[¶5] DON'T CHASE A LOGO AND LOSE A GREAT FIT. This is where families can get themselves into trouble. They have a school that: Wants their daughter. Fits her list. Fits academically. Fits financially. Fits basketball-wise. Has coaches she trusts. And gives her a real opportunity. But they keep waiting because: "Maybe something bigger will come." Then the school that wanted her fills the spot. And the "better" opportunity never comes. Now you're not choosing between two schools. YOU LOST ONE OF THEM.
[¶6] THIS DOESN'T MEAN RUSH. Do your homework. Take the visits. Ask the hard questions. Look at finances. Understand the role. Talk as a family. You should never commit somewhere simply because you're afraid. But once you have the information you need... DON'T CONFUSE WAITING WITH MAKING A BETTER DECISION. Sometimes waiting creates more options. Sometimes it costs you the best one you already had. DON'T TURN DOWN SOMEONE WHO HAS MADE YOU A PRIORITY JUST TO CHASE SOMEONE WHO HASN'T. Know what you want. Know where you stand. Know the timeline. And when you find the right fit... DON'T BE SO BUSY LOOKING FOR "BETTER" THAT YOU LET "RIGHT" WALK AWAY.
