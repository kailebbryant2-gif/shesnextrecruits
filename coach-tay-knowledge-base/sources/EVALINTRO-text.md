[¶1] Before you watch the video, I want you to understand one very important thing: Evaluation is about so much more than points and winning.
[¶2] Yes, coaches care about whether your daughter can play. But they are evaluating the entire player.
[¶3] They are watching how she moves, communicates, competes, responds to mistakes, interacts with teammates, handles adversity, plays without the ball, listens to coaching, and much more.
[¶4] We will go much deeper into what coaches are actually looking for throughout the athlete sections of this course.
[¶5] But for now, understand this: EVALUATION HAPPENS FAST. And it starts much earlier than most families realize.
[¶6] Every interaction counts. From the moment a coach sees her at a tournament, they are forming impressions. That can include what happens on the court, on the bench, during warmups, between games, and even how she carries herself around the gym.
[¶7] A coach may only watch your daughter for a short period of time. That means you don't get to decide when the evaluation starts. It starts when the coach starts watching.
[¶8] In the video below, I'll walk you through what coaches are actually looking for when they evaluate a player and how quickly that evaluation can happen.
[¶9] Don't worry about memorizing everything yet. We'll break down the specific things coaches evaluate throughout the rest of this course.
[¶10] For now, just remember: It's never just about the points. Everything counts.
