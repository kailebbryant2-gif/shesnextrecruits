[¶1] As recruiting starts to progress, add a second tab to the same Google Sheet. Your first tab can stay: COACH CONTACT TRACKER. Then create: OFFERS & VISITS. This tab is for the schools that are moving beyond basic outreach and into more serious recruiting conversations.
[¶2] I would track: SCHOOL; COACH; OFFERED? — YES / NO; DATE OFFERED; OFFER DETAILS; UNOFFICIAL VISIT? — YES / NO; UNOFFICIAL VISIT DATE; OFFICIAL VISIT? — YES / NO; OFFICIAL VISIT DATE; NOTES / NEXT STEP.
[¶3] Your notes might say: Waiting on decision after visit. Coach wants family back on campus. Offer expires August 1. Need financial aid breakdown. Official visit scheduled for September. Still deciding between three schools.
[¶4] WHY KEEP THIS SEPARATE? Because eventually you don't need your most important recruiting information buried inside a giant contact list. Your first tab answers: WHO HAVE WE CONTACTED? Your second tab answers: WHERE IS THIS ACTUALLY GOING? As offers and visits begin happening, this second tab gives your family a quick picture of where recruiting actually stands.
[¶5] And once you're getting closer to making a decision, we'll add even more information about each school so you can actually compare your options instead of just counting offers. DON'T JUST TRACK WHO KNOWS YOUR NAME. TRACK WHO IS MOVING THE RELATIONSHIP FORWARD.
