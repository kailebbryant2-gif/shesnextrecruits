# SIGNALS-F · "Recruiting Reality: What Coaches Say and Do" (image, transcribed)

- **Source:** Image pasted by the user, 2026-11-11 (Batch 4). Subtitle: "A quick guide to help you read the signals." Footer: "Pay attention to both what they say and what they do. Consistency shows priority." Tagline: "Know the signs. Make informed decisions. Play your next level."

| IF THEY'RE REALLY INTERESTED ("They'll say it. They'll show it.") | IF THEY'RE NOT (OR JUST KEEPING YOU ON THE LIST) ("They'll be vague. They'll do less.") |
|---|---|
| **Clear communication:** "You're a priority for us." | **Generic messages:** "Keep working hard. We'll keep you in mind." |
| **Frequent contact:** "We'll be in touch often and keep you updated." | **Inconsistent contact:** "We'll reach out when we have more information." |
| **Invite you to campus early:** "We'd love to get you on campus soon." | **Don't prioritize a visit:** "Maybe we can get you on campus sometime in the future." |
| **Come watch you play:** "We'll be at your next game/tournament." | **Rarely (or never) come watch:** "We're still evaluating and hope to see you at some point." |
| **Talk about a specific role:** "We see you fitting in as a ___." | **Keep it general:** "You could be a good fit for our program." |
| **Include you in their plans:** "You're part of what we're building." | **Avoid specifics:** "It's a little early to talk about that right now." |
| **Call (not just text):** "We want to build a real relationship with you." | **Mainly text (if they respond at all):** "👍 Sounds good, keep in touch!" |
| **Be direct about next steps:** "Here's what we need from you and our timeline." | **Don't give a clear timeline:** "We'll let you know as things get closer." |
